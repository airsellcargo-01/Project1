How your air-cargo shipment moves

We confirm the shipment details and airline routing before cargo is received at HGA.

Request a quote

Send the destination, cargo-ready date, pieces, weight, dimensions, and commodity details.

Confirm booking

We confirm the available routing, price, cut-off time, and cargo-delivery instructions.

Prepare at HGA

Deliver your cargo to Airsell for documentation, weighing, measurement, marking, and labelling.

Tender to the airline

We coordinate the e-AWB data and tender the cargo to the airline or its handling agent.

Airline acceptance

The airline completes final acceptance. We then confirm the accepted shipment status to you.

Track to destination

We provide updates until arrival at the destination airport for consignee collection.
